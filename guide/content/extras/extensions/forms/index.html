Forms

Contact, booking and quote forms for your pages. A form service receives the messages and emails them to you; many have a free plan for a small website.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Some people would rather write than call, at any hour. A form also asks for exactly what you need to answer: the date, the number of guests, the kind of project.

A website made of files cannot send email by itself. A form service such as Formspree or Web3Forms does it for you: you create a free account and get an address for your form.

Write a form

One text file per form in content/forms/, one line per field: its label, its type and whether it is required.

---
title: Book a table
---
Your name | text | required
Email | email | required
Date | date | required
Guests | select | required | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
Anything we should know? | textarea

Types: text, email, tel, number, date, time, textarea, select, radio, checkbox and consent, a box that must be ticked, with a link to your privacy policy.

Show it and connect it

  1. Put the form into a page with its name in capitals: book.txt is {{FORM_BOOK}.
  2. Put your form service's address into content/forms/_index.html, after action:. The README shows the settings for Formspree and Web3Forms.
  3. Build, put the website online and send yourself a test message.

Until the address is there, the form says that it is not connected yet, instead of losing messages. For sensitive matters, such as health or legal problems, ask people to call you instead.

Add it

  1. Download extension-forms.zip and unzip it. Move the folder forms into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds an example contact form in content/forms/, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
