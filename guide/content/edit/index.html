Make it yours

Change anything, then build again

Every change works the same way: edit a file, save it, run bash build.sh, and reload the page in your browser.

To edit the files, use a plain text editor. Visual Studio Code is free and works on every computer. Word or Pages will not work, because they add their own formatting.

Your name, contact and menu: site.conf

Open site.conf. It holds the settings for the whole website. Change the text between the quotes:

SITE_NAME="Atrium"
EMAIL="hello@example.com"
PHONE="+1 555 0142"

No spaces around the = sign. If something is wrong, Shingle tells you which line to fix.

The menu has one line per link: the text you see, a |, and the address of the page.

MENU=(
  "About|/about/"
  "Services|/services/"
  "Contact|/contact/"
)

Each setting has a short explanation next to it in the file.

The text of a page: content/

Each file in content/ is one page: content/about.html becomes the About page. Change the words between the tags and leave the tags as they are:

<h1>Calm, bright rooms that are built to last</h1>
<p>Write your own text here.</p>

The lines at the very top, between ---, are the page's title and description for search engines and link previews.

A new page

  1. Copy content/about.html and name the copy, for example, content/prices.html.
  2. Change its title at the top and its text.
  3. Add "Prices|/prices/" to MENU in site.conf.

Pictures

Put your pictures in static/img/ and show them in a page like this:

<img src="/img/our-team.jpg" alt="Our team in front of the office">

The alt text describes the picture for people who cannot see it. About 1600 pixels wide is plenty; save photos as JPG.

Your logo and icon

  • static/img/logo.svg is the logo next to your name. A logo.png works too (then delete logo.svg).
  • static/favicon.svg, favicon.ico and apple-touch-icon.png are the small icons in the browser tab and on phones.
  • static/img/share.png (1200 × 630 pixels) is the picture shown when someone shares your website.

Colors and fonts

Colors and fonts come from a theme. Pick another one in Themes, or change the colors in themes/default/theme.css.
