Privacy policy

What information this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

This is a sample policy for a small business website. Adapt it to your own business and have it checked by someone who knows the law in your country.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to {{SITE_NAME}}, {{ADDRESS}}. If you have a question about your data, write to {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

When you visit the website, our hosting provider keeps short technical logs: your IP address, the page you opened, the time and your browser type. These logs help to keep the website secure and are deleted automatically after a few days.

When you write to us, we keep your email and what you send us, so we can answer you and, if we work together, run your project. We do not use it for anything else and we do not add you to a mailing list.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.

Fonts

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page opens, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which can see your IP address. If you prefer that this does not happen, you can block the domain fonts.googleapis.com in your browser.

Theme setting

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored in your own browser (in “local storage”). It never leaves your device and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.

How long we keep it

Messages from people who do not become clients are deleted after one year. Project emails and files are kept for as long as the law requires, usually ten years after the project ends.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to {{EMAIL}} and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
