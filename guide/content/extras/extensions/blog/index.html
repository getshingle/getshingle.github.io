Extensions

Extension

Blog

Articles with a cover picture, newest first, 12 to a page, and a feed so readers can follow you in a news reader.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Advice, behind-the-scenes stories and answers to the questions your clients ask. Articles bring visitors from search engines, and show that you know your work.

Write a post

Each post is a file in content/blog/. Its name starts with the date and ends with the post's address:

content/blog/2026-10-18_opening-day.html            → /blog/opening-day/
content/blog/2026-10-18_opening-day/index.html      a post with pictures:
content/blog/2026-10-18_opening-day/cover.jpg       the picture on the blog page
---
title: We are open
summary: One or two sentences for the blog page and link previews.
tag: News
author: anna-berg
cover_alt: Our new shop from the street
---
<p>After three months of work, the doors are open.</p>
  • draft: true keeps a post off the website; bash build.sh preview shows drafts.
  • A post dated in the future appears with the first build on or after that day.
  • author with the address of a person from the team extension links to their page.

Add it

  1. Download extension-blog.zip and unzip it. Move the folder blog and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example posts in content/blog/ and a Blog link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
