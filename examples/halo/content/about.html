About the studio

A small studio for websites that last

Halo Studio was founded in 2015 by a designer and a developer who were tired of websites that looked good in the pitch and fell apart a year later. Today we are twelve people, and we still build every site as if we will be the ones looking after it. Usually we are.

Small on purpose

We work on four or five projects at a time. That keeps the same senior people on your project from the first workshop to the launch, and it means we answer your emails ourselves.

Most of our work comes from clients who come back, or who send their friends.

Where we work

Our studio is on Mill Street in Riverton, and about half of the team works remotely across Europe. We meet clients in person for the first workshop whenever we can, and on video after that.

We work in English, German and French.

What we believe

Plain words first

A website is mostly text. We write and structure the content before we design around it, and we avoid jargon in our sites and in our meetings.

Speed is a feature

Every second of loading time loses visitors. We keep pages light, images small and scripts to the minimum the page needs.

Accessible by default

One in five people has a disability of some kind. Building for them from the start costs little; fixing it later costs a lot.

No lock-in

We use open, well-known tools and hand over everything at the end: code, accounts, passwords and documentation.

Work with us

The first call is free and comes with honest advice, even if the advice is that you do not need us.
