Your job openings, each with its own page and an apply button. Open positions show up in Google's job search.

What it is for

When you hire. The careers page tells people why to work with you, and each position has the details and a way to apply.

Add it

  1. Download extension-careers.zip and unzip it. Move the folder careers and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example jobs in content/careers/ and a Careers link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
