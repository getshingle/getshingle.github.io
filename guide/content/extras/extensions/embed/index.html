Embed

Videos, maps and booking calendars from YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Calendly and others. They load only when a visitor presses the button.

Download See it ↗

Why only after a click?

A video or map from another website loads that website's files and cookies with your page, before anyone has watched anything. That makes pages slow, and in the EU it needs the visitor's consent. Embed shows a placeholder with a button instead; nothing loads until the visitor presses it. YouTube videos then come from youtube-nocookie.com.

Add one

One text file per video, map or calendar in content/embed/:

---
title: A walk through the studio
link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…
---

link is a YouTube or Vimeo address, a Calendly link, or the embed code that Google Maps or OpenStreetMap give you under "Share", pasted on one line.

Put it into a page with its name in capitals: map.txt is {{EMBED_MAP}, studio-tour.txt is {{EMBED_STUDIO_TOUR}.

Add it

  1. Download extension-embed.zip and unzip it. Move the folder embed into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds an example map and video in content/embed/, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
