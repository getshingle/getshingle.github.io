The challenge

Northpass makes an app for planning hiking routes. After five years of quick growth, their app and website used twelve shades of green, seven button styles and three different fonts.

What we did

  • Audited every screen and collected every color, button and form
  • Designed a compact design system with clear rules for when to use what
  • Built the components in code, with documentation for designers and developers
  • Redesigned the marketing website using the new system

The result

The team now builds new screens from shared parts, which has made design reviews shorter and the product more consistent. The new website was built in five weeks.
