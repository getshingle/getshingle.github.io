Themes

Colors and fonts

A theme sets the colors, fonts and corners of your website, in light and dark mode. Your pages stay exactly as they are.

Default ↗

Concrete, oak and black iron: warm limestone pages, iron-black buttons and oak details, with wide Archivo headings and square corners. Comes with Shingle, so there is nothing to download.

Forest ↗

Calm forest green on pale stone, with wide Archivo headings. Made for studios, architects and makers.

Ink and violet on white, set in Geist, with large tight headings and round buttons. Made for studios and startups.

Clay ↗

Terracotta on warm white, set in Instrument Sans, with round buttons. Warm and friendly; suits cafés, shops and craftspeople.

Download

Use a theme

  1. Copy

    Download the theme, unzip it and move its folder, for example forest, into themes.

  2. Choose

    In site.conf, change the theme line to THEME="forest".

  3. Build

    Run bash build.sh and reload your website.

Make your own

  1. Copy the folder themes/default and call the copy, for example, themes/mine.
  2. Set THEME="mine" in site.conf.
  3. Open themes/mine/theme.css and change the colors. Each color has two values, one for light mode and one for dark mode: 
    --accent: light-dark(#80532a, #d8a873);    /* links and highlights: oak */
  4. For other fonts, pick a font on Google Fonts, copy its link into the line Fonts: at the top of theme.css, and put its name in --font-body and --font-heading.

Check that text stays easy to read in both modes: the moon or sun button at the top right switches between them.
