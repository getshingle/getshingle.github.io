Add an extension

  1. Download and unzip

    Download the extension and double-click the zip. You get a folder with the extension inside, for example gallery, and for some extensions two more files, collection.sh and collection.css.

  2. Copy into extensions

    Move everything from that folder into the extensions folder in your shingle folder. If your computer asks whether to replace collection.sh or collection.css, say yes.

  3. Build

    Run bash build.sh. The first time, Shingle puts example content in content/ and adds a link to the menu in site.conf. It tells you what it added.

  4. Make it yours

    Change or replace the examples. Each extension's page here explains how, and its README.md explains every field.

On any page

Many extensions also give you a value to put into any of your pages, in double curly brackets, the same way as {{EMAIL}}:

{{SERVICES}}your services as cards
{{PRICING}}your plans
{{SCHEDULE}}the weekly timetable
{{TESTIMONIALS}}three quotes from clients
{{FAQ}}questions and answers, or one topic: {{FAQ_FEES}}
{{DOWNLOADS}}the list of files
{{OPENING_HOURS}}your opening hours
{{FORM_CONTACT}}the form in content/forms/contact.txt
{{EMBED_MAP}}the map in content/embed/map.txt

For example, testimonials on your home page: open content/index.html and add a section:

<section>
  <h2>What our clients say</h2>
  {{TESTIMONIALS}}
</section>

Switch one off

Remove the link from the menu

Delete its line from MENU (or LEGAL) in site.conf. Shingle adds the link only the first time, so it will not come back.

Switch an extension off for a while

Rename its folder so it starts with _, for example extensions/_careers, and delete its menu line. Your content stays in content/careers/; rename the folder back to switch it on again.

Remove an extension completely

Delete its folder in extensions/, its folder in content/, its menu line, and its values from your pages.
