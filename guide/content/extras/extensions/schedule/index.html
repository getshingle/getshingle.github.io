Extension

Schedule

A weekly timetable: one column per day on a wide screen, one day below the other on a phone.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Classes in a gym or a yoga studio, courses in a school, training times in a club, the day in a kindergarten. The columns do not have to be days: rooms, groups or months work the same way.

Write a day

One text file per day in content/schedule/, in the order of their numbers:

---
title: Monday
---
07:00-08:00 | Morning flow | Maja
18:00-19:15 | Vinyasa | Maja | Studio 1
Closed in the afternoon

One line per class: the time, the name and any details such as the teacher, the room or the level, separated by |. A line without | is a note.

On other pages

{{SCHEDULE} shows the timetable on any page. The page's title and text are in content/schedule/_index.html.

Add it

  1. Download extension-schedule.zip and unzip it. Move the folder schedule into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds an example week in content/schedule/ and a Schedule link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
