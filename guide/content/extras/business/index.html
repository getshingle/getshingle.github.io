What a website of files does not do

A Shingle website is a folder of finished pages: fast, safe and cheap to host, because nothing runs on a server. For the few things that need a server, use a service made for it and link to it, or show it with an extension:

You needUseOn your website
Messages from a formA form service, such as Formspree or Web3FormsThe forms extension
Online bookingA booking calendar, such as Calendly or Cal.com, or the system you already useEmbed, or the button of a service
Table reservationsYour reservation systemA link from the menu button, such as MENU_BUTTON="Book a table|https://…"
Payments, vouchers, a few productsPayment links, such as Stripe Payment LinksThe buttons of pricing or services
A large online shopA shop platformA link in the menu; your website tells the story
A newsletterA newsletter service; most give a sign-up form addressThe forms extension with that address
Videos and mapsYouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, OpenStreetMapThe embed extension
Paid courses and member loginsA course or membership platformLinks from services or pricing

Whatever you connect, mention it in your privacy policy.

Every website, whatever the business

Three extensions are worth adding to almost any website.

Business

Your opening hours on your pages, and your address and hours for Google and maps.

Sitemap

A list of all your pages, so search engines find every one of them.

Privacy policy

A sample policy to adapt, linked at the bottom of every page.
