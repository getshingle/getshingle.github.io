Team

A card for each person, and a page for each with their photo, their story, contact details and social links.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

People choose people: a doctor, a lawyer, a teacher, a trainer. Show who they will meet, with a real photo and a few words of their own.

Add a person

content/team/001_anna-berg/index.html   → /team/anna-berg/, first on the team page
content/team/001_anna-berg/photo.jpg    a portrait, 4:5 (for example 800 × 1000)
content/team/004_james-okafor.html      no photo: their initials are shown
---
title: Anna Berg
role: Founder and principal architect
summary: One sentence for the team page.
phone: +1 555 0110
email: anna@example.com
linkedin: https://www.linkedin.com/in/…
---
<p>Their story.</p>

Leave out any field you do not need. Email addresses are hidden from spam robots.

Add it

  1. Download extension-team.zip and unzip it. Move the folder team and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example people in content/team/ and a Team link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
