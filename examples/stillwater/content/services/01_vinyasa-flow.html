Each class flows through standing poses, balances and backbends, timed with your breath, and ends with a long rest. The teacher shows easier and harder versions of every pose, so beginners and old hands practise side by side.

Good to know

  • Come with an empty stomach: eat at least two hours before.
  • Bring water. You will need it.
