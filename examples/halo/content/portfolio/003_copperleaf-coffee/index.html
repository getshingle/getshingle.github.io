The challenge

Copperleaf roasts coffee in a small workshop and sold it mainly at markets. They wanted to sell subscriptions online, but they had no time to manage a complicated shop.

What we did

  • Refreshed the brand: a simple logo, warm colors and photos taken in the roastery
  • Built a small online shop with one-off orders and monthly subscriptions
  • Automated shipping labels and reminder emails
  • Wrote the product pages with the roasters, in their own words

The result

Subscriptions now make up most of the roastery’s sales, and the shop takes about an hour a week to run.
