--- title: Yin yoga summary: Long, quiet holds on the floor that open the hips and the back. Slow, deep and calming. level: All levels length: 60 minutes book: /schedule/ book_label: See the schedule ---

In yin yoga you stay in a few floor poses for three to five minutes each, supported by bolsters and blocks. It stretches the tissue around your joints and calms the mind. A good balance to running, cycling or a busy week.
