Nina studied graphic design and spent seven years at a large digital agency, where she led design for banks and public services. She founded Halo Studio in 2015 to work on fewer projects, with more care, for clients she could get to know.

She runs the discovery workshop at the start of every project and stays involved until launch. Her favorite part of the job is the moment a client’s team realises they can update the site themselves.

She writes and speaks about accessible design, and still sketches every new site on paper first.
