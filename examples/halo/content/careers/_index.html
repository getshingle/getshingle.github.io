Working at Halo

We work in small teams of two or three, with the same people on a project from start to finish. Everyone talks to clients, and everyone’s name is on the work.

Half of us work from the Riverton studio and half work remotely, mostly within two hours of Central European Time. We meet in person four times a year.

What we offer

  • A permanent contract and a salary range published in every job ad
  • A four-day week, Monday to Thursday, on full pay
  • 25 days of paid holiday, plus public holidays
  • A laptop of your choice and €1,500 a year for courses, books and conferences
  • Time for open-source work and writing on the studio’s blog

How we hire

  1. Send us a short note and your CV, with links to two or three things you have made.
  2. A first call of about 45 minutes, to get to know each other.
  3. A paid task of about four hours, based on a real project, which we discuss together.
  4. A final conversation with two people from the team, and an answer within a week.
