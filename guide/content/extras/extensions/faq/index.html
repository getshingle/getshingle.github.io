Extensions

Extension

FAQ

Questions and answers on a page of their own, grouped by topic, and on any other page where they help, such as your prices.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Every question you answer on the website is one less phone call, and a reason less not to book. Search engines read the page as questions and answers, too.

Add a question

One file per question in content/faq/; the number at the start sets the order:

---
title: Do you charge for the first meeting?
topic: Fees
---
<p>No. The first hour is free and without obligation.</p>

topic is optional; on the FAQ page, each topic gets a heading.

On other pages

{{FAQ} shows every question on any page. {{FAQ_FEES} shows only the questions with topic: Fees: the topic in capitals, with _ for spaces.

Add it

  1. Download extension-faq.zip and unzip it. Move the folder faq into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example questions in content/faq/ and an FAQ link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
