The challenge

Fernhill Dental Care has three dentists and one receptionist, who spent most of the morning on the phone booking appointments. The practice wanted patients to book online, without making the website feel like a booking form.

What we did

  • Designed a calm, friendly site with real photos of the practice and the team
  • Connected the practice’s booking system, so free times show directly on the site
  • Wrote clear pages for nervous patients, children and emergencies
  • Made every page work well on a phone, where most patients visit

The result

Six months after launch, four in ten appointments are booked online, most of them outside opening hours. The receptionist now has time for the patients in the waiting room.
