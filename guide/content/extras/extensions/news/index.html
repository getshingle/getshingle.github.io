Extensions

Extension

News

Short announcements as a dated list, newest first, with a feed: new opening hours, an award, a new colleague, an event.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

For things that are true today and old next month. Use the blog for longer articles that stay useful, and news for short updates.

Write an announcement

content/news/2026-10-01_new-opening-hours.html   → /news/new-opening-hours/
---
title: New opening hours from November
summary: We now open at 8:00 on weekdays.
---
<p>The text, as long or short as you like.</p>

A folder with index.html and cover.jpg gives an announcement a picture.

Add it

  1. Download extension-news.zip and unzip it. Move the folder news and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example news in content/news/ and a News link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
