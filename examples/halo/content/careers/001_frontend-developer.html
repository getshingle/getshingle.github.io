We are looking for a front-end developer with at least three years of experience who enjoys building things that are quick, solid and pleasant to use.

  • Keep pages light, accessible and fast on every device

What we are looking for

  • Very good HTML and CSS, and solid JavaScript
  • Experience with accessibility, or a strong wish to learn it properly
  • Experience with at least one static site generator or modern framework
  • Clear written English; German or French is a plus

What we offer

A four-day week on full pay, 25 days of holiday and a yearly learning budget. The salary for this role is €58,000 to €70,000 a year, depending on experience.
