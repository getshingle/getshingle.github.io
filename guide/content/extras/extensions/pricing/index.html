Extension

Pricing

Plans or packages side by side, with one that stands out and, if you like, a switch between monthly and yearly prices.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Memberships and subscriptions, software plans, and packages of a photographer, a coach, a cleaner or an agency. Two to four plans fit side by side; on a phone they stack.

Write a plan

One file per plan in content/pricing/; the number at the start sets the order.

---
title: Unlimited
summary: As many classes as you like.
price: €89
period: per month
price_yearly: €74
period_yearly: per month, paid yearly
featured: true
badge: Best value
button: Start today
link: /contact/
---
<ul>
  <li>Every class, every day</li>
  <li>Bring a friend once a month</li>
</ul>

The list becomes the plan's features, each with a check mark. When one plan has a price_yearly, a Monthly/Yearly switch appears above the plans. featured: true gives a plan a frame and a filled button.

On other pages

{{PRICING} shows the plans on any page, such as your home page. Text below the plans, such as "All prices include tax" or your questions about prices, goes into content/pricing/_index.html.

Add it

  1. Download extension-pricing.zip and unzip it. Move the folder pricing and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example plans in content/pricing/ and a Pricing link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
