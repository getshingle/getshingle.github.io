Business

Your opening hours on your pages, and your business details for Google and maps: name, address, phone, hours and social links.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Every shop, restaurant, practice and workshop. People look for your hours more than for anything else, and search engines show them next to your name when they know them.

Your hours and details

They go into content/business/_index.html, which is not a page:

---
type: Dentist
monday: 8:00-18:00
tuesday: 8:00-18:00
wednesday: 8:00-12:00, 14:00-19:00
thursday: 8:00-18:00
friday: 8:00-14:00
saturday: closed
sunday: closed
hours_note: Closed on public holidays.
street: 12 Harbour Lane
postal_code: RV1 4AB
city: Riverton
country: GB
---

type is what search engines should call you, such as Restaurant, Dentist, HairSalon or Store; the README lists many more. Your name, phone and social links come from site.conf.

On your pages

  • {{OPENING_HOURS}: a table of your hours, for example on the contact page.
  • {{OPENING_HOURS_TEXT}: the same in one line. In site.conf, FOOTER_TEXT="Open {{OPENING_HOURS_TEXT}}." shows it at the bottom of every page.
  • {{MAP_URL}: a link to your place on a map.

Add it

  1. Download extension-business.zip and unzip it. Move the folder business into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds content/business/_index.html, where your hours and details go, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
