We are looking for a product designer who is as happy talking to users as working in a design tool.

What you will do

  • Run interviews and usability tests with our clients’ users
  • Design web apps and websites, from sketches to detailed interfaces
  • Grow and maintain our design system
  • Work closely with developers until everything ships

What we are looking for

  • At least four years of experience designing digital products
  • A portfolio that shows how you think, not only how things look
  • Good knowledge of accessibility guidelines
  • Comfortable presenting your work to clients

What we offer

A four-day week on full pay, 25 days of holiday and a yearly learning budget. The salary for this role is €60,000 to €72,000 a year, depending on experience.
