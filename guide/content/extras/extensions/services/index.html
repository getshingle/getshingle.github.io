Extension

Services

What you offer, as cards on a services page and a page for each service, with its price, duration and a button to book it.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Anything you sell or do: treatments, classes and courses, rooms, packages, practice areas, repairs. Each service gets a card and its own page, so you can explain it properly and link to it from anywhere.

Write a service

One file per service in content/services/. The number at the start sets the order. A folder holds the service's picture too:

content/services/01_facial/index.html   → /services/facial/
content/services/01_facial/cover.jpg    its picture
content/services/02_massage.html        without a picture
---
title: Facial treatment
summary: Cleansing, a mask and a massage for tired skin.
price: €65
duration: 60 minutes
book: https://cal.com/your-salon/facial
book_label: Book a time
---
<p>What happens, what is included and who it is for.</p>

Every field you add, such as price or duration, appears as a fact under the title, and the first two on the card. Use your own words in any language: cena: 65 € works too. book is where the button goes: a booking page, your contact page or an email link.

On other pages

{{SERVICES} shows all the cards on any page, for example on your home page.

If your website already has a page content/services.html, the extension's page replaces it. The build tells you, so you can move what you want to keep into content/services/_index.html.

Add it

  1. Download extension-services.zip and unzip it. Move the folder services and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example services in content/services/ and a Services link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
