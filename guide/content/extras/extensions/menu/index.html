Extension

Menu

Your menu or price list: sections such as Breakfast and Drinks, each dish with its price, a short description and tags such as V for vegetarian.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Restaurants, cafés and bars, and just as well the price list of a hair salon, a garage or a workshop. You write it as plain text, one dish per paragraph, so changing a price takes seconds.

Write a section

One text file per section in content/menu/, in the order of their numbers:

---
title: Breakfast
note: Until 11:30
---
Granola with yoghurt | 7.50 | V
Toasted oats and seeds, seasonal fruit, honey.

Eggs on sourdough | 9.50 | V
Two poached eggs, spinach and hollandaise.

Espresso | 2.40

The first line of a dish is its name, its price and its tags, separated by |. The lines below it are the description. Leave an empty line between dishes. Write prices the way you want them shown: 9.50, €9.50 or 9,50 €.

The page's title, text and legend, which explains the tags, are in content/menu/_index.html.

A price list instead of a menu? Rename the folder extensions/menu to extensions/prices before the first build: the page is then at /prices/, with its text in content/prices/.

Add it

  1. Download extension-menu.zip and unzip it. Move the folder menu into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds an example menu in content/menu/ and a Menu link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
