Portfolio

Your projects, each with a case study page: the client, the year, the place, and the story with pictures. Or a simple list of clients and references.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Architects, designers, agencies, builders and consultants: show what you have done, and let each project tell its story. Investors can list their companies the same way.

Write a project

content/portfolio/001_riverside-house/index.html   → /portfolio/riverside-house/
content/portfolio/001_riverside-house/cover.jpg    the main picture
---
title: Riverside House
client: The Lind family
year: 2025
location: Riverton
services: Architecture, interiors
summary: One or two sentences for the card and link previews.
cover_alt: What the cover picture shows
---
<h2>The challenge</h2>
<p>The case study: the challenge, what you did, the result.</p>

Only a list, without a page for each project? Write pages: false in content/portfolio/_index.html.

Add it

  1. Download extension-portfolio.zip and unzip it. Move the folder portfolio and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example projects in content/portfolio/ and a Projects link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
