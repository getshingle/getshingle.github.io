Extension

Cloudflare

Two small files for websites on Cloudflare Pages (or Netlify): safe settings for browsers, and redirects for pages that moved.

Download

Looking for how to put your website on Cloudflare? That is in Free hosting with Cloudflare. This extension is a small extra for later, once your website is there.

Why you need it

  • _headers: safer and faster. It tells browsers not to guess file types, not to show your pages inside other websites, and to keep your stylesheet and script, which Shingle renames when they change.
  • _redirects: no broken links. When you rename a page or move from an old website, old links and search results still lead to the right page instead of "not found".

Use it

The files are in extensions/cloudflare/static/. Add a line to _redirects for each page that moved, from the old address to the new one:

/about-us/        /about/        301
/old-blog/*       /blog/         301

Other web hosts ignore both files, so they do no harm there.

Add it

  1. Download extension-cloudflare.zip and unzip it. Move the folder cloudflare into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds _headers and _redirects to every build, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
