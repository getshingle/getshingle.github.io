Privacy policy

A sample privacy policy at /privacy/, linked at the bottom of every page, written to be adapted to your business.

Download See it ↗

Why you need one

In the EU and in many other countries, a website that collects personal data must say what it collects, why, for how long and who else sees it. That includes a contact form, an email address people write to, statistics about visitors, and videos or maps from other websites. A Shingle website itself sets no cookies and collects nothing, which keeps the policy short.

A clear policy also builds trust: people see that you take their data seriously.

Make it yours

The page is in content/privacy/index.html. Replace the sample company with yours, delete what you do not do, and add the services you use, such as your form service or a booking calendar.

It is a sample, not legal advice. Have it checked by someone who knows the law in your country, especially if you handle health, children's or financial data.

Add it

  1. Download extension-privacy.zip and unzip it. Move the folder privacy into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds the page in content/privacy/index.html and a link at the bottom of every page, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
