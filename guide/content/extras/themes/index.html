Themes

Colors and fonts

A theme sets the colors, fonts and corners of your website, in light and dark mode; three are always dark. Your pages stay exactly as they are. Each demo opens in a new tab.

Use a theme

  1. Copy

    Download the theme, unzip it and move its folder, for example forest, into themes.

  2. Choose

    In site.conf, change the theme line to THEME="forest".

  3. Build

    Run bash build.sh and reload your website.

Make your own

  1. Copy the folder themes/default and call the copy, for example, themes/mine.
  2. Set THEME="mine" in site.conf.
  3. Open themes/mine/theme.css and change the colors. Each color has two values, one for light mode and one for dark mode: 
    --accent: light-dark(#80532a, #d8a873);    /* links and highlights: oak */
  4. For other fonts, pick a font on Google Fonts, copy its link into the line Fonts: at the top of theme.css, and put its name in --font-body and --font-heading.

Check that text stays easy to read in both modes: the moon or sun button at the top right switches between them.
