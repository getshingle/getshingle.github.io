How a project works

A typical website takes six to ten weeks. You see working pages from the second week, not a big reveal at the end.

  1. Discovery

    A workshop to agree on goals, audience and content. You get a fixed quote and a schedule.

  2. Design

    We design in the browser with your real content, so you judge the site as visitors will see it.

  3. Build

    Weekly releases you can click through, with speed and accessibility checked on every one.

  4. Launch and care

    Redirects, analytics and training for your team, then a monthly care plan if you want one.
