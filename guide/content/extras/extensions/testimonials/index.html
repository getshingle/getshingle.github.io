Testimonials

What your clients say, as quote cards with their name, a photo or their initials, and stars if you like. On your home page, or wherever they help.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Words from real clients do what your own words cannot. Put three on the home page and near your prices, where people decide.

<p>What they said, in their words.</p>

A folder with index.html and photo.jpg adds a photo. Only publish what people really said, with their permission.

On your pages

Put {{TESTIMONIALS} where the quotes should be, for example on your home page. It shows the first three; show: 6 in content/testimonials/_index.html shows more. The page /testimonials/ shows them all.

Add it

  1. Download extension-testimonials.zip and unzip it. Move the folder testimonials and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example quotes in content/testimonials/, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
