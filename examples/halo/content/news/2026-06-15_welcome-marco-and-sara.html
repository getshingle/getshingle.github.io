--- title: Welcome, Marco and Sara summary: Two new colleagues joined the studio this spring, and we are now twelve people. ---

This spring Marco Rossi joined us as a front-end developer and Sara Lindqvist as a project manager. With them, Halo Studio is now twelve people, half in the Riverton studio and half working remotely across Europe.

Marco will lead the front-end work on our larger websites. Sara takes over planning and day-to-day contact for most new projects, so that our designers and developers can spend more of their time designing and developing.
