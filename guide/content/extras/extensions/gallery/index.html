Extension

Gallery

Photo albums, each with its own page, and photos on the gallery page itself. A photo grows out of its small picture to fill the screen; the arrow keys go to the next one.

Download See it ↗

What it is for

Restaurants and hotels show their rooms, photographers their weddings, builders their work before and after, salons their results. Good photos sell more than any text.

Add photos

content/gallery/garden.jpg                          on the gallery page
content/gallery/01_summer-wedding/index.html        an album → /gallery/summer-wedding/
content/gallery/01_summer-wedding/01_ceremony.jpg   its photos, in the order of their names
content/gallery/01_summer-wedding/01_ceremony.txt   a caption, if you like

The album's index.html has its title and a one-line summary. Its cover is cover.jpg, or else its first photo.

  • Name your photos with words: ceremony-under-the-oak.jpg becomes the description "Ceremony under the oak" for people who cannot see it. A caption file replaces it.
  • Make them smaller first: 2000 pixels on the long side is plenty. Photos straight from a camera make the page slow.

Add it

  1. Download extension-gallery.zip and unzip it. Move the folder gallery and the two files collection.sh and collection.css into the extensions folder of your website. If your computer asks whether to replace the two files, say yes.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds example photos in content/gallery/ and a Gallery link to the menu, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
