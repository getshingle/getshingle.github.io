--- title: Riverton Public Library's new website is live summary: After four months of research, design and testing with library visitors, the new site launched this week. cover_alt: The library's new home page with opening hours and upcoming events ---

The Riverton Public Library’s new website went live on Monday. It replaces a site from 2014 that visitors described, kindly, as “a maze”.

We spent the first month with library staff and visitors, finding out what people actually come to the site for. The answer was short: opening hours, events, renewing a book and finding the right branch. The new site puts those four things on every page.
