Extension

Sitemap

A list of every page of your website for Google and other search engines (sitemap.xml), and the robots.txt file that points them to it.

Download See it ↗

Why you need it

Search engines find pages by following links, which takes time and can miss pages that few links point to. A sitemap hands them the full list at once. It matters most for a new website, and whenever you add pages.

After your website is online, add it in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit https://www.your-domain.com/sitemap.xml. They then show you how people find you.

What you do

Only one thing: set SITE_URL in site.conf to your real address, such as SITE_URL="https://www.example.com". Every build makes:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<urlset xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
  <url><loc>https://www.example.com/</loc></url>
  <url><loc>https://www.example.com/about/</loc></url>
  …
</urlset>

To give search engines your own rules, put your own robots.txt into static/; the extension then leaves it alone.

Add it

  1. Download extension-sitemap.zip and unzip it. Move the folder sitemap into the extensions folder of your website.
  2. Run bash build.sh. The first time, the build adds sitemap.xml and robots.txt to every build, and tells you so.
  3. Change the examples to your own and build again.

The extension's README, also in the download, explains every field and how to change the look.
