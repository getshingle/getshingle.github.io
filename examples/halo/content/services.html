Services

From the first workshop to years of care

Every project gets a fixed price per phase, agreed before the phase starts. No hourly surprises.

Websites

Company websites, product sites and content-heavy sites with a blog, news or a knowledge base. We plan the content with you, design it in the browser and build it so your team can update it without calling us.

Typical project: 6 to 10 weeks, from €12,000.

What is included

  • Discovery workshop and content plan
  • Design of every page type, on desktop and phone
  • Development, speed and accessibility testing
  • Moving your existing content and redirects
  • Training for your team and written documentation

Web apps

Booking systems, client portals, dashboards and internal tools. We start with the people who will use the app, test early prototypes with them and build in small steps you can try every week.

Typical project: 3 to 6 months, from €40,000.

What is included

  • User interviews and a clickable prototype
  • Interface design and a small design system
  • Development with automated tests
  • Security review and a privacy checklist
  • Launch, monitoring and a support period

Questions we are often asked

How long does a website take?

Most company websites take six to ten weeks from the first workshop to launch. The biggest factor is usually content: sites with content ready on time launch on time.

Which platform do you build on?

It depends on who updates the site and how often. Many of our sites are static sites with a simple editor, which makes them fast, cheap to host and hard to hack. For large teams we also build on WordPress and on headless content systems.

Who owns the website?

You do: the code, the design files, the domain and every account. We hand everything over at launch, whether or not you choose a care plan.

Can you write the content?

We plan the structure of every page and edit what you write. For larger sites we work with two freelance writers we trust and include them in the quote.
