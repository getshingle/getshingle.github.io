About us

A smokehouse that found its fire again

Ember opened in 2019 in the old smokehouse by the harbour, where fish was smoked for almost a hundred years.

We kept the brick walls and the iron hooks, and built the kitchen around a wood fire. Almost everything we cook passes through it: vegetables in the embers, fish on the grill, bread in the hot oven at the end of the night.

We buy from a handful of farms within an hour's drive and from the boats that land in the morning. When something runs out, it is gone from the menu; you will find something else in its place.

The wine list is short and changes as often as the food. We drink everything on it ourselves, and we are happy to open a bottle for a glass.

The room

Forty seats, a bar of eight and one long table. In summer, the terrace on the quay.
