Free and open source

Make a website with one command

Shingle turns a folder of pages into a fast, finished website. No database, no account, and on most computers nothing to install.

Download Shingle See a demo ↗ How it works

Three steps

You get a complete demo website. Change the words and pictures, and it is yours.

  1. Download

    Download Shingle and double-click the zip. You get a folder called shingle.

  2. Build

    Open a terminal in that folder and type bash build.sh.

  3. Look

    Open the new dist folder and double-click index.html. That is your website.

Get started

What you get

A complete website

Home, about, services and contact pages with real text to replace, a menu, a footer, and a light and dark mode.

Fast and accessible

Plain pages that load quickly on any phone, work with a keyboard and a screen reader, and do not track your visitors.

Yours to keep

The result is a folder of ordinary files. Put it on any web host, or for free on GitHub and Cloudflare.

Add more when you need it

The basic website stays small. Everything else is an extra you add later, by copying one folder.

Extensions

A blog, projects, news, your team, job openings, a privacy policy and more.

Themes

Other colors and fonts, in light and dark mode. Change one line to switch.

Examples

Complete websites for other kinds of business, ready to make your own.
