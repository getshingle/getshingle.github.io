Put it online

Your whole website is one folder

After bash build.sh, the folder dist holds your complete website: pages, pictures and styles. Anything that can show files on the internet can show it.

Before you start, set your real address in site.conf, for example SITE_URL="https://www.example.com", and build again.

Choose your way

Step 1 is all most people need. The other steps are for when you want them, one at a time.

  1. Your own web host

    You already pay for hosting or a domain with a web space. Upload the contents of dist and you are done.

  2. Keep it on GitHub

    Free. Every version of your website is saved, and you can publish with two clicks.

  3. Free hosting with Cloudflare

    Cloudflare shows your website from GitHub, fast and free, with your own domain if you have one.

  4. Let the internet build it

    For later: change a page on GitHub, and your website builds and updates itself.
